Section 04 — About

Working at the intersection of design and code.

Mike Gilbert is a designer and developer based in Baltimore, working on the web team at Inside Lacrosse, where he moves between building the product and shaping the editorial work that runs on it.

Outside of that, he builds CollegeSoccer.io independently — concept, design, and engineering — and keeps a running list of smaller experiments in Labs, because building things he's curious about is half the point.
